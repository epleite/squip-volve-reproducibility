# Data licensing and attribution

The **software** in this repository is released under the MIT License. The MIT License does **not** apply to Volve-derived data.

The small ASCII tables in `data/compact/` are derived from the publicly released Equinor Volve field dataset. They are provided only as reviewer-facing scientific benchmark inputs and remain subject to the **Equinor Open Data Licence** and its attribution/use conditions. They are not relicensed under MIT by this repository.

Before reusing or redistributing any Volve-derived table, users must review the current official Equinor terms at the Volve data-sharing page. The repository does not grant rights beyond those provided by Equinor and the Volve licence partners.

Raw Volve seismic volumes, the complete prepared fences, and original public well files are **not redistributed** here. `docs/VOLVE_DATA.md` documents the source and preparation boundary.

The CSV files in `data/results/` are manuscript analysis outputs. Where a result contains quantities derived from Volve, the underlying Equinor attribution/use obligations still apply. Purely synthetic/no-data mathematical outputs are generated by the authors' software.

For citation/attribution in scientific use, cite both the associated GEOPHYSICS manuscript and the official Equinor Volve field dataset.
